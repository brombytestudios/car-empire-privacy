Privacy Policy for the app “Car Empire”

Android app by Brombyte Studios, package name com.carempire.game · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short

  • No account, no server of ours. Your save game and settings stay on your device. We ourselves receive no personal data from you.
  • You can play offline. The app uses an internet connection only for optional rewarded videos, for purchases through Google Play and when you open a link.
  • Data leaves your device only through third-party services: advertising (Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads) and purchases (Google Play). Sections 4 and 5 explain what is transmitted.

2. Data on your device

2.1 Save game

Your save game is the file save.json in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It holds your progress: in-game currencies (money, medals, trophies, legend stars), the plant, research, managers, shares, loans and joint ventures, statistics, timestamps (for offline earnings and reminders), the company name you chose, which one-time purchases you own, and the order numbers of your most recent purchases (at most 64, so that no purchase is credited twice).

2.2 Settings

The app stores your language, sound and music, vibration, battery saver, day-night cycle, notifications on or off (and whether the permission has already been requested) and your choice about personalised advertising.

The app processes this data only locally, to provide the game to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We have no access to it.

2.3 Deleting

In the game, Menu → “Reset all progress” deletes your company, trophies, medals and epic research; the settings remain. Uninstalling the app deletes all of the app's data. Your one-time purchases (starter pack, “Ad-free” and design packs) can be restored through Google Play afterwards.

2.4 Android backup

If you have enabled backup to your Google account in Android, Android may back up the app's data (save game and settings) to Google Drive automatically and restore it on a new device. This is a feature of Android and Google, controlled under Settings → Google → Backup. Google's privacy policy applies to it.

3. Notifications

With your permission the app shows reminders: empty parts silos, the daily gift, the next weekly event, a season door, the end of a contract (one hour before), a test drive coming back and the end of a joint venture. The app schedules these reminders locally on your device when you leave it. There is no push server and no data is transmitted. After a device restart, pending reminders are scheduled again.

The game asks for the notification permission on its own at most once (Android 13 and newer): only when reminders help – after a longer absence or when you accept a long contract – and only after briefly explaining what for. You can ask again yourself with the switch in the menu. You can switch reminders off at any time in the game under Menu → “Notifications” or in the Android settings under Apps → Car Empire → Notifications.

4. Advertising: Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads

4.1 What we offer

Car Empire shows only optional rewarded videos: double offline earnings, medals (up to ten videos a day), a free boost and manager chests (up to three videos a day). There are no forced ads, no banners and no interruptions. A video starts only when you tap an ad button.

If you bought “Ad-free”, you get these rewards without a video; only the manager chests stay a video. The ad SDK still stays in the app and keeps running as described in section 4.3.

4.2 Providers

The videos are mediated by Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation, technically the ironSource SDK). Unity Ads is currently the only ad network integrated. Both services are operated by Unity Technologies Inc., San Francisco (USA), including its subsidiary ironSource Ltd., Tel Aviv (Israel).

4.3 When the ad SDK runs

When the app starts, the ad SDK first sets itself up on your device only: it creates its own storage files in the app's private storage and keeps track of which screen is currently active. It does not transmit any data at this point. It connects to the ad servers only after you have answered the question “Personalised ads?”, which appears once after the tutorial. From then on it preloads videos in the background, even if you never watch one. The technical data listed in section 4.4 is therefore transmitted even if you never start a video.

4.4 Data that is transmitted

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and, derived from it, an approximate location at country or region level,
  • device and network information: manufacturer, model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier,
  • app information: package name and version,
  • your interactions with ads: video started, completed, tapped,
  • technical diagnostics of the SDK (errors, loading times),
  • according to Unity Ads, also information about in-app purchases and an SDK-internal user identifier.

With your consent this data is also used for personalised advertising, that is, for interest profiles. The SDK may also use the Android advertising services of the Privacy Sandbox (Topics, Attribution Reporting).

4.5 Purposes

Delivering ads and attributing your reward, limiting how often ads are shown, settlement with the ad network, detecting fraud and abuse, error analysis and statistics. With your consent, additionally the personalisation of ads.

4.6 Legal bases

Personalised advertising: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future in the game under Menu → “Personalised ads”.
Non-personalised advertising: our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4.7 Transfers to third countries

Unity processes data in the USA and in Israel. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). For Israel an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR).

4.8 Retention

Retention periods are set by Unity. Details are in Unity's privacy policy for game players and app users.

4.9 Your choices

  • Withdraw consent: switch off Menu → “Personalised ads”.
  • Reset or delete the advertising ID: in the Android settings under Google → All services → Ads (on some devices Privacy → Ads). Without an advertising ID the SDK receives only a string of zeros.
  • Tap the privacy icon in a running ad to open the privacy options of Unity Ads.
  • Request access or deletion from Unity through the form named in Unity's privacy policy linked above. We ourselves store none of this data and therefore can neither view nor delete it.

5. In-app purchases through Google Play

Medal packs, the starter pack, the piggy bank, the “Ad-free” purchase and the design packs are bought through Google Play's billing system. Google handles the payment (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). We receive no payment data, no name and no address.

From Google Play the app receives only the product bought, the order or transaction number, the time and the state of the purchase. The order number is stored in your save game so that no purchase is credited twice. After a reinstall, Google Play tells the app which one-time purchases (starter pack, “Ad-free”, design packs) you own, so that they are restored. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Your purchase history and refunds are managed by Google in your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies to them.

6. Google Play and Android

Google Play may collect crash and performance data of the app (Android Vitals) through your Google account if you have allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics data. We have no influence over this; Google's privacy policy applies. The app uses no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

8. Children and young people

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. In Germany, consent to personalised advertising can only be given validly by persons aged 16 and over (Art. 8 GDPR). Younger players should choose “No, thanks” or ask their parents.

9. Your rights

Towards us you have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), to erasure (Art. 17), to restriction of processing (Art. 18), to data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77), for example with the authority responsible for us: the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany, www.lda.bayern.de.

Since we ourselves store no personal data about you, requests about advertising data go to Unity (section 4.9) and requests about purchases go to Google (section 5). Feel free to write to us anyway, and we will help you along.
