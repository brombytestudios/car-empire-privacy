Privacy Policy for the app “Car Empire”

Android app by Brombyte Studios, package name com.carempire.game · Last updated: 28 October 2026 (version 0.8.0)

In short

  • No account needed, you can play offline. Your save game and settings stay on your device.
  • Online features are optional. Only when you go online under “Rankings & friends” do we store your company name, your scores and a factory card through Unity Gaming Services, so that rankings, friends and gifts work (section 6). You can delete this in the game at any time.
  • Google Play Games: if you are signed in there, the app backs up your save game in your Google account and reports achievements (section 7).
  • Other third-party services: advertising (Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads, section 4) and purchases as well as the “Car Empire Plus” subscription (Google Play, section 5).

2. Data on your device

2.1 Save game

Your save game is the file save.json in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. It holds your progress: in-game currencies (money, medals, trophies, legend stars, research and race points), the plant, research, managers, shares, loans and joint ventures, statistics, timestamps (for offline earnings and reminders), the company name you chose, which one-time purchases you own and until when a subscription runs, and the order numbers of your most recent purchases (at most 64, so that no purchase is credited twice). For the online features it also holds: whether you take part online and when you accepted the rules, the player IDs of players you blocked, redeemed bonus codes, the state of a referral and your shares in other players' companies (with their player ID and company name).

2.2 Settings

The app stores your language, sound and music, vibration, battery saver, day-night cycle, the compact bar, notifications on or off (including the daily calendar's reminder, and whether the permission has already been requested) and your choice about personalised advertising.

The app processes this data locally to provide the game to you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We have no access to it. What leaves the device when you use the online features or Google Play Games is described in sections 6 and 7.

2.3 Deleting

In the game, Menu → “Reset all progress” deletes your company, trophies, medals and epic research; the settings remain. You delete your online data under Menu → “Privacy & data” → “Delete online data” (section 6.9). Uninstalling the app deletes all of the app's data on the device. Your one-time purchases (starter pack, “Ad-free” and design packs) and a running subscription can be restored through Google Play afterwards.

2.4 Android backup

If you have enabled backup to your Google account in Android, Android may back up the app's data (save game and settings) to Google Drive automatically and restore it on a new device. This is a feature of Android and Google, controlled under Settings → Google → Backup. Google's privacy policy applies to it. The cloud save through Google Play Games is described in section 7.

3. Notifications

With your permission the app shows reminders: an empty parts depot, the daily gift, the next weekly event, a season door, the end of a contract (one hour before), a test drive coming back, the end of a joint venture and the end of a training in the staff office. The app schedules these reminders locally on your device when you leave it. There is no push server and no data is transmitted. After a device restart, pending reminders are scheduled again.

The game asks for the notification permission on its own at most once (Android 13 and newer): only when reminders help – after a longer absence or when you accept a long contract – and only after briefly explaining what for. You can ask again yourself with the switch in the menu. You can switch reminders off at any time in the game under Menu → “Notifications”, the daily gift reminder also with the switch in the daily calendar, or in the Android settings under Apps → Car Empire → Notifications.

4. Advertising: Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads

4.1 What we offer

Car Empire shows only optional rewarded videos: double offline earnings, medals (up to ten videos a day), a free boost and manager chests (up to three videos a day). There are no forced ads, no banners and no interruptions. A video starts only when you tap an ad button.

If you bought “Ad-free” or your “Car Empire Plus” subscription is running, you get these rewards without a video; instead of the chest videos you then get manager vouchers. The ad SDK then does not connect to the ad servers: when the app starts it only sets itself up on your device (section 4.3), loads no videos and does not transmit the data listed in section 4.4; the question “Personalised ads?” is not asked. If you buy “Ad-free” while the SDK is already connected, it loads no further videos and no longer connects from the next start of the app. When your subscription ends, it connects again as described in section 4.3.

4.2 Providers

The videos are mediated by Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation, technically the ironSource SDK). Unity Ads is currently the only ad network integrated. Both services are operated by Unity Technologies Inc., San Francisco (USA), including its subsidiary ironSource Ltd., Tel Aviv (Israel).

4.3 When the ad SDK runs

When the app starts, the ad SDK first sets itself up on your device only: it creates its own storage files in the app's private storage and keeps track of which screen is currently active. It does not transmit any data at this point. It connects to the ad servers only after you have answered the question “Personalised ads?”, which appears once after the tutorial – and only if you have neither “Ad-free” nor a running “Car Empire Plus” subscription (section 4.1). From then on it preloads videos in the background, even if you never watch one. The technical data listed in section 4.4 is therefore transmitted even if you never start a video.

4.4 Data that is transmitted

  • your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and, derived from it, an approximate location at country or region level,
  • device and network information: manufacturer, model, operating system version, language, time zone, screen size, network type and mobile carrier,
  • app information: package name and version,
  • your interactions with ads: video started, completed, tapped,
  • technical diagnostics of the SDK (errors, loading times),
  • according to Unity Ads, also information about in-app purchases and an SDK-internal user identifier.

With your consent this data is also used for personalised advertising, that is, for interest profiles. The SDK may also use the Android advertising services of the Privacy Sandbox (Topics, Attribution Reporting).

4.5 Purposes

Delivering ads and attributing your reward, limiting how often ads are shown, settlement with the ad network, detecting fraud and abuse, error analysis and statistics. With your consent, additionally the personalisation of ads.

4.6 Legal bases

Personalised advertising: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future in the game under Menu → “Personalised ads”.
Non-personalised advertising: our legitimate interest in financing the free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4.7 Transfers to third countries

Unity processes data in the USA and in Israel. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it, and otherwise on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR). For Israel an adequacy decision of the European Commission exists (Art. 45 GDPR).

4.8 Retention

Retention periods are set by Unity. Details are in Unity's privacy policy for game players and app users.

4.9 Your choices

  • Withdraw consent: switch off Menu → “Personalised ads”.
  • Reset or delete the advertising ID: in the Android settings under Google → All services → Ads (on some devices Privacy → Ads). Without an advertising ID the SDK receives only a string of zeros.
  • Tap the privacy icon in a running ad to open the privacy options of Unity Ads.
  • Request access or deletion from Unity through the form named in Unity's privacy policy linked above. We ourselves store none of this advertising data and therefore can neither view nor delete it.

5. Purchases and subscription through Google Play

Medal packs, the piggy bank, the “Ad-free” purchase, the design packs and the “Car Empire Plus” subscription are bought through Google Play's billing system. Google handles the payment (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). We receive no payment data, no name and no address.

From Google Play the app receives only the product bought, the order or transaction number, the time and the state of the purchase, and for the subscription also whether and until when it runs. The order number is stored in your save game so that no purchase is credited twice; for the subscription the app only remembers until when it is valid. After a reinstall, Google Play tells the app which one-time purchases (starter pack, “Ad-free”, design packs) and which running subscription you have, so that they are restored. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Purchase reports to Unity: The purchasing library in the app (Unity In-App Purchasing by Unity Technologies Inc., USA) also reports every purchase to Unity: purchase started, paid, failed and completed. A report contains the product with price and currency, the Google Play receipt (order number, purchase token and signature), device model, Android version, language settings, app version, install time, and a session and an installation identifier from Unity; if you have gone online (section 6), also your player ID. Unity receives your IP address in the process. Unity collects this telemetry for its purchasing service; the built-in version of the library cannot switch it off. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in reliable purchase processing (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For the transfer to the USA, section 4.7 applies; details are in Unity's privacy policy for game players and app users.

The “Car Empire Plus” subscription renews automatically every month. You can cancel and manage it at any time in Google Play under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions or in the game through Store → Car Empire Plus → “Manage”. Your purchase history, subscriptions and refunds are managed by Google in your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies to them.

6. Online features: rankings, friends and gifts (Unity Gaming Services)

6.1 Optional

Rankings, friends, gifts, factory postcards, shares in players' companies, bonus codes and referrals are optional. They start only when you go online in the game under “Rankings & friends” and accept the rules for company names. Before that, the app sends nothing to Unity Gaming Services.

6.2 Your online account

When you join, Unity Authentication creates an account for you with a random player ID; you need no name, e-mail address or password. If you are signed in to Google Play Games (section 7), the app links this account with your Play Games account: Google gives Unity a one-time code for this, and Unity stores the player ID of your Play Games account. That way you find your online account again on a new device. When signing in, the app also sends Unity's installation identifier, a random identifier of this app installation.

6.3 Data that is stored

  • your player ID and your company name (visible to others, see 6.4),
  • your scores in the rankings: best company value, trophies and cars built, plus the most recently checked values and their time,
  • a factory card: best car model, company value, trophies, legend stars, cars built, your best manager (kind, rarity, level), the levels of your halls, transporters, parts depot, harbour and airport, and the paint of your best model,
  • your friend code, your friends list, open friend requests and players you blocked (Unity Friends),
  • gifts: which friends you sent something to today and which gifts are waiting for you,
  • redeemed bonus codes; for a referral, the friend code of the player who invited you and whether the reward is due or paid; as an inviter, the number of your referrals,
  • reports (6.5): the player ID of the reported player, their company name at the time of the report, the player IDs of the reporters and the time.

With every connection Unity also processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address; according to Unity, the Friends and Leaderboards services derive an approximate location from it. Your scores are not written by the app itself but by a checking script on Unity's server that rejects implausible values.

6.4 Who sees what

All players who are online see your company name and your scores in the rankings. Friends also see your factory card and the factory postcard of your plant. Whoever enters your friend code sees your company name in the request. In the stock exchange, friends and players among the top 20 of the company value ranking can buy shares in your company; only your public company value is used for this, and it costs you nothing. Other players never see your player ID, your Google name or your e-mail address.

6.5 Rules, word filter, reporting and blocking

Company names are checked with a word filter on the device and on the server; a match is shown to others as “Company” with a number. You can report another player's name and block the player. If at least three different players report the same name, it is hidden for everyone until we have checked it. For the check we see only the reported company name and the player IDs. Blocked players are kept in a list on your device and, as a block, in Unity Friends.

6.6 Provider and transfers to third countries

The online features run on Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Leaderboards, Friends) of Unity Technologies Inc., San Francisco (USA). Unity processes the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR) in data centres in the USA; a location in the EU cannot be chosen. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where Unity is certified under it, and otherwise on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

6.7 Purposes and legal bases

We process this data to provide the online features you chose (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The word filter, reports and blocking protect the other players and serve compliance with Google Play's rules for user-generated content; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a safe player community (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6.8 Retention

We store the online data for as long as you use the online features and delete it when you delete it (6.9) or ask us to. Reports about a player are deleted once we have checked them or the reported player deletes their online data. If a name stays hidden after the check, only the player ID remains in a list of hidden players until that player deletes their online data.

6.9 Deleting online data

In the game under Menu → “Privacy & data” → “Delete online data” (or in the “Rankings & friends” area) you delete at once: your factory card and your scores in all rankings, your friend code, your friendships, gifts, redeemed codes, your referral data, reports about you and your account at Unity. Reports you made about others remain, with your player ID that then belongs to no account any more, until we have checked them. If you no longer have the app, follow the steps on our deletion page.

7. Google Play Games: sign-in, cloud save and achievements

If Google Play Games is set up on your device, Google signs you in there when the app starts. The app receives your Play Games player ID from Google. When you are signed in, the app uses two Google services:

  • Cloud save (Saved Games): the app stores a copy of your save game (section 2.1) with a short description (car model and company value) in an area of your Google Drive reserved for the game, at most every 15 minutes and when you leave the game. On a new device the app offers to load it. Under Menu → “Cloud save” you see the last backup and can back up or load yourself.
  • Achievements: when you reach one of the achievements in the game, the app reports it to your Play Games profile.

According to Google, it also collects your gamer identity (gamertag, avatar) and analytics and diagnostics data to keep the Play Games services stable. The provider is Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA). The legal basis is providing these features (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We ourselves have no access to this data. What Google stores and how you delete your Play Games profile, saved games and achievements is governed by Google; you find the settings in Google Play Games and in your Google account. Google's privacy policy applies. If you do not want this, sign out of Google Play Games; the game then runs without cloud save and achievements.

8. Sharing, help and e-mails to us

Share my stats: the button in the menu opens Android's share sheet with a short text: company name, best car model, company value, trophies, your friend code (only if you are online) and a link to the store listing. The text goes only where you share it; we receive nothing of it.

E-mails to us: “Report a bug” and the contact buttons of the help sheet (“A purchase did not arrive”, “My game is lost”) open your e-mail app with a prepared message. It contains the app version, device model, Android version, language and a short game summary (car model, number of company sales, save game version). The app sends nothing itself; you see the e-mail before sending it and can change or delete anything. We use your e-mail address and the content only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete the message once your request is settled and no statutory retention obligation applies.

9. Google Play and Android

Google Play may collect crash and performance data of the app (Android Vitals) through your Google account if you have allowed Android to share usage and diagnostics data. We have no influence over this; Google's privacy policy applies. The app uses no analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.
