Delete the online data of “Car Empire”

App “Car Empire” by Brombyte Studios on Google Play, package name com.carempire.game · Last updated: 24 October 2026

Car Empire needs no account with a name, e-mail address or password. An online account only exists if you go online in the game under “Rankings & friends”. This page explains how to delete that online account and all data that belongs to it.

Way 1: in the game (at once)

  1. Open Car Empire and tap the menu at the bottom right (9 dots).
  2. Scroll to “Privacy & data”.
  3. Tap “Delete online data” and confirm with “Delete”.

The same button is in the “Rankings & friends” area. The deletion happens at once; your game on the device keeps running offline.

Way 2: without the app (by e-mail)

Write to BrombyteStudios@gmail.com with the subject “Car Empire: delete online data”. So that we can find your online account, please give us your friend code (in the game under “Rankings & friends” → “Friends”) and your company name. We delete the data without undue delay, at the latest within one month, and confirm it by e-mail.

What is deleted

  • your online account at Unity Gaming Services (player ID and, if present, the link with Google Play Games),
  • your company name and your scores in all rankings (company value, trophies, cars built),
  • your factory card (car model, company value, trophies, legend stars, best manager, levels, paint),
  • your friend code, your friendships, friend requests and blocks,
  • sent and waiting gifts,
  • redeemed bonus codes and your referral data,
  • reports by other players about you and your entry in the list of hidden names.

What remains and for how long

  • Reports you made about others: they remain, with your player ID that then belongs to no account, until we have checked them, and are deleted afterwards.
  • Your save game on the device: it stays until you uninstall the app or choose “Reset all progress” in the game.
  • Data at Google: purchases and subscriptions are managed by Google in your Google account; you delete the cloud save and the achievements of Google Play Games in the settings of Google Play Games or of your Google account. We have no access to them.
  • Advertising data at Unity: you request access and deletion from Unity, see the privacy policy, section 4.9.
